Why fractional

Get the leverage before
you add the headcount.

A Fractional Chief of Staff isn't a smaller version of a full-time hire. It's senior operating support focused on you and the work that creates the most leverage.

What can you actually accomplish fractionally?

The point isn't to fill 30 hours. It's to change what happens beyond them.

Part of the work is building systems that keep working without constant input. You're buying leverage, not activity.

Can you understand my business if you're not here full-time?

Fractional doesn't mean peripheral.

WellBuilt works remotely, directly with you and across your leadership team. That means getting inside the priorities, people, decisions and dependencies that shape the company.

Will you actually own anything?

You're not buying advice. You're buying senior ownership.

A conversation with your Chief of Staff shouldn't leave you with more work to do.

What happens when something urgent comes up?

The engagement flexes around the business, not a rigid weekly timesheet.

Some weeks need more time than others. The 30 hours flex within each month around what the business needs. Hours don't roll over or accrue.

Why wouldn't I just hire a full-time Chief of Staff?

At some point, you might.

A full-time Chief of Staff makes sense when there's enough ongoing work to justify the hire. Fractional gives you senior capability before that point, without adding permanent headcount too early.

Questions

The questions founders ask
before we work together.

What is WellBuilt in one sentence?

A Fractional Chief of Staff who takes work off the founder, then builds the systems that stop recurring work coming back.

How is this different from a typical fractional Chief of Staff?

A traditional fractional Chief of Staff helps run the work. WellBuilt also identifies recurring work that can be removed, redesigned or automated, then builds and embeds the right workflows.

The difference isn't more activity. It's more leverage.

What role does AI actually play?

You hire the person.

WellBuilt operates as your Fractional Chief of Staff first. Where recurring work can be redesigned or automated safely, those workflows get built and embedded. The judgement, accountability and relationship stay human.

I already have an EA, COO and leadership team. Where does WellBuilt fit?

Your EA helps manage your day. Your Chief of Staff reduces how much of the company needs to reach your day in the first place.

Your COO owns operating performance. The CoS doesn't replace them. It reduces the cross-company coordination that still has to happen through you.

Your functional leaders own their areas. WellBuilt works across the gaps between them.

How does the six-month engagement work?

Because you can't redesign what you don't yet understand.

The first 90 days are spent taking ownership and learning where dependency and friction sit. The next 90 are spent building and embedding what that context reveals.

Three months would mean stopping just as the highest-leverage changes become clear.
