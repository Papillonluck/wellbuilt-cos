01 / The Founder Bottleneck

As the company grows,
the way you run it has to change.

In a consumer startup, the founder holds an enormous amount of the business: the product, the customer, the commercial context, the priorities and the decisions that keep everything moving.

As the company grows, that dependence on you doesn’t simply disappear into the team. More people, priorities and dependencies create a different kind of complexity.

06 / Why fractional

Get the leverage before
you add the headcount.

A Fractional Chief of Staff isn't a smaller version of a full-time hire. It's senior operating capability focused on you and the work that creates the most leverage.

What can you actually accomplish fractionally?

The point isn't to fill the hours. It's to change what happens beyond them.

Part of the work is solving what's in front of you. Part is building the systems, decisions and ways of working that keep things moving without constant founder input. You're buying leverage, not activity.

Can you understand my business if you're not here full-time?

Fractional doesn't mean peripheral.

WellBuilt works remotely, directly with you and across the business. That means getting inside the priorities, people, decisions and dependencies that shape the company. You don’t need someone sitting beside you five days a week. You need someone close enough to know what matters and senior enough to know what to do about it.

Will you actually own anything?

You're not buying advice. You're buying senior ownership.

That means taking work off you, driving it forward and bringing decisions back when they genuinely need you. A conversation with your Chief of Staff shouldn't leave you with more work to do.

What happens when something urgent comes up?

The engagement flexes around the business, not a rigid daily schedule.

WellBuilt works with you for 10 hours a week. How that time is used flexes around what the business needs. Some weeks are predictable. Others aren't. The capacity is there for both.

Why wouldn't I just hire a full-time Chief of Staff?

At some point, you might.

A full-time Chief of Staff makes sense when the role has enough ongoing work to justify permanent headcount. Fractional gives you senior capability before that point, without hiring ahead of the need.

08 / Questions

The questions founders ask
before we work together.

What is WellBuilt in one sentence?

A Fractional Chief of Staff who takes ownership across the business, helping you build, launch and move critical work forward without everything routing back through you.

As the business develops, that also means redesigning and automating the work that shouldn’t need your attention at all.

How is this different from a typical fractional Chief of Staff?

A traditional fractional Chief of Staff helps run the work. WellBuilt also identifies recurring work that can be removed, redesigned or automated, then builds and embeds the right workflows.

The difference isn't more activity. It's more leverage.

What role does AI actually play?

You hire the person.

WellBuilt operates as your Fractional Chief of Staff first. Where recurring work can be redesigned or automated safely, those workflows get built and embedded. The judgement, accountability and relationship stay human.

I already have an EA, COO and leadership team. Where does WellBuilt fit?

Your EA helps manage your day. Your Chief of Staff reduces how much of the company needs to reach your day in the first place.

Your COO owns operating performance. The Chief of Staff doesn't replace them. It reduces the cross-company coordination that still has to happen through you.

Your functional leaders own their areas. WellBuilt works across the gaps between them.

How does the 90-day minimum work?

The first 90 days are about getting properly inside the business and taking ownership.

WellBuilt learns the priorities, people and decisions, and identifies where founder dependency can be removed or the way the business operates should change.

After 90 days the engagement becomes rolling. The aim isn’t to keep you tied into a fixed term. The best fractional relationships often start with a defined need and continue because the person becomes a valuable part of how the business operates. From there, the work can expand into building and embedding the systems and AI automations that the first 90 days have shown are worth building.
